## Inclusion criteria

Every entry is active (updated in the last 6 months), has real content (not a scraper, an ad shell or paid-promotion-only), and was checked by hand. Labels mean the same thing in every table:

| Label | ✅ | ⚠️ | ❌ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Free | Core features are free | Some features are paid | Must pay |
| Accepts submissions | Free submission | Paid-only, or invite-only | Does not accept submissions |
| China access | Homepage loads within 10 seconds on a mainland China network | Loads, but slow or unreliable | Does not load |
| English UI | Full English interface | Partly English | Chinese only |

China access is tested by hand on a mainland China network and re-tested every month. Full details are in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
