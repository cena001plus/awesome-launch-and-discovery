# What are the best Product Hunt alternatives?

Product Hunt is the best-known place to launch a tech product, but it isn't the only one. The alternatives below differ in three ways: how often they launch new products, what a free submission costs you in waiting time or backlinks, and who is reading. Last checked 2026-10-19. Labels follow the [main list](../README.md#inclusion-criteria).

## How to choose

**Daily or weekly.** On a daily site (Product Hunt, Uneed, Fazier) a launch competes for one day, so you get a short spike and a ranking. Weekly sites (Peerlist Launchpad, OpenHunts, TinyLaunch, DevHunt) keep you on the board for a week, which suits makers who can't rally votes in a single day. Microlaunch runs month-long cohorts.

**What "free" costs.** Most of these sites have a free path with conditions, and the conditions change often. As of October 2026:

- Uneed gives free launches a date 30 days to 5 months out. Product Hunt and Peerlist Launchpad have no paid queue.
- Fazier and OpenHunts ask for a badge or backlink on your site in exchange for a free listing, or a faster one.
- BetaList no longer has a free option.

Check the submit page before you plan a launch date.

**Who is reading.** Show HN and DevHunt reach developers, so they work best for developer tools and technical products. Indie Hackers and r/SideProject reach other makers, which is useful for feedback more than for customers. Product Hunt has the broadest audience of tech early adopters.

**Chinese users.** None of the overseas sites target Chinese readers. To reach them, launch on a platform in China as well, with a Chinese description. The [launch in China guide](launch-in-china.md) covers how. Whether each overseas site loads in mainland China is shown in the China access column.
