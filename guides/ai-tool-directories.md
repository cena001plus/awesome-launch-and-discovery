# What are the best AI tool directories?

AI tool directories help you find AI tools by task. They also give makers a place to list a new tool. The ones below are active, have real listings, and were checked by hand on 2026-10-19. Labels follow the [main list](../README.md#inclusion-criteria).

## How to choose

**To find a tool for a task**, start with There's An AI For That, which is searchable by task, or Futurepedia, which is organized by category. FutureTools is smaller and hand-picked. Hugging Face Spaces is different from the rest: the apps run right in the browser, so you can try a model before you commit to anything.

**To see what's popular**, AICPB ranks AI apps and websites by usage, with separate charts for China and worldwide. Toolify also ranks tools.

**To list your own AI tool**, know that the largest directories now charge for listings. As of October 2026:

- There's An AI For That, Toolify, Futurepedia, AI-bot.cn and AIbase have no free listing.
- FutureTools is free, but it rejects most submissions and doesn't list tools that are still on a waitlist.
- AI Agents Directory lists agents for free if you add its badge or backlink to your site.

**For MCP servers**, the official MCP Registry, Glama and Smithery are all free to publish to. The official registry is meant to be the upstream source that other registries and clients build on, but it is still in preview.

**For Chinese users**, AI-bot.cn and AIbase are the large Chinese-language directories, and AIbase also has an English interface.
