# Where should I launch my product?

There's no single best place to launch. Where you should go depends on what you built and who it's for. This page groups the platforms in the [main list](../README.md#launch-your-product) by product type, then covers what to prepare before you submit. Last checked 2026-10-19.

## Before you submit

**Write one description and reuse it.** Prepare a name, a one-line description and a short paragraph, and use the same wording everywhere. Readers recognize the product when they see it twice, and search engines and AI assistants link the mentions together more easily.

**Have something people can try.** Several platforms turn away products that can't be used yet:

- SaaSHub rejects unreleased products and waitlists.
- FutureTools doesn't list waitlists.
- Show HN asks for something readers can try, not a landing page or a fundraiser.

If you're still pre-launch, BetaList and Launching Next are built for that stage.

**Check the account rules.** Peerlist Launchpad needs a verified profile. Product Hunt doesn't allow company accounts. Most community sites expect the maker to post and to answer comments on launch day.

**Plan around queues.** Free paths often mean waiting. As of October 2026:

- Uneed's free line runs 30 days to 5 months.
- OpenAlternative publishes one free listing per weekday, which works out to a wait of a few months.
- AlternativeTo's free review backlog is a few months.

Submit early, or check whether the paid fast-track is worth it to you.

**Spread it out.** Launching on every site in the same week splits your attention. Pick one main launch, such as Product Hunt or Show HN, and add the directories over the following weeks.
