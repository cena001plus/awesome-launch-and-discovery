# How do I launch a product in China?

Launching to Chinese users means using different platforms, writing in Chinese, and checking a few practical details that overseas launches don't need. This guide covers all three. The platforms come from the [China section](../README.md#china) of the main list, last checked 2026-10-19.

**Picking a starting point:**

- **Developer tools and side projects:** V2EX's 分享创造 ("share your creations") board is where Chinese developers post their own work for feedback.
- **Indie websites and apps:** the Chinese Independent Developer list on GitHub adds new projects every day.
- **Open source:** HelloGitHub and OSChina.
- **Media coverage:** 36Kr and ifanr both take pitches through a form.
- **Ruan Yifeng's weekly** is a Chinese tech newsletter whose GitHub repository has over 100,000 stars, and it takes self-submissions as GitHub issues.

## Language

Most of these platforms are Chinese-only (see the English UI column), and so are their readers. Before you submit:

- **Write the description in Chinese**, ideally with help from a native speaker. Machine translation reads as machine translation.
- **Decide on a Chinese name, or decide to keep the English one.** Many products keep an English name, and that's fine. Either way, use the same name on every platform.
- **Use the Chinese words people search for.** For example, Chinese users say 独立开发 for indie development and 效率工具 for productivity tools.

## Practical details

- **Sign-up.** Many Chinese platforms ask for a mainland China phone number when you sign up or post. Check this before your launch day.
- **Payments.** Chinese users mostly pay with WeChat Pay or Alipay. A product that only takes international cards will lose many of them at checkout.
- **Hosting.** A website hosted on a server in mainland China needs an ICP filing (备案) before it can go live. A site hosted outside mainland China doesn't need one, but it may load slowly for users in China, so test it from a mainland network.
- **Distribution.** In China, Android apps are distributed through phone makers' app stores and other local stores, not Google Play. Many products also ship as a WeChat Mini Program (小程序) alongside their app or website.
- **Platform rules.** Read each board's rules before posting:
  - V2EX's 分享创造 is for your own creations.
  - Appinn asks software that's sold only as a paid product, or costs more than ¥100 a year, to use its paid promotion.
  - Builderoom accepts only work the submitter helped make.
